# Citadel facade completion: current recipe fixed point

Scope: unwired source composition only. This is not composer integration,
publication, rendered appearance, collision appearance, navigation, gameplay,
performance, or headed acceptance.

The current opening-head and lower-facade recipes are composed atomically by
`CitadelFacadeCompletionRecipe`. Production eligibility is derived from sealed
facade aperture declarations plus fresh structured physical checks identifying
an uncommitted bottom panel as structural mass that does not reach a root. No
seed, source artifact, gate count, test offset, named panel list, or candidate
SHA participates in production policy.

`DeterministicRecipeCompletion` processes one sorted eligible panel at a time,
re-discovers eligibility after each accepted change, exhausts only explicit
monotone geometry blockers, continues past blocked work, and propagates all
schema/work/infrastructure failures atomically. `exhausted` is distinct from
`fullyResolved`; this candidate is exhausted with three unsupported panels.
Monotone rejection is evidence-based rather than reason-string based: nested
work-limit, malformed-schema, and invalid-measurement results are hard failures,
while a foreign-solid rejection requires a valid measured collision and an
overlap rejection requires concrete intersecting bounds. Socket exhaustion
accounts for every attempted placement and retains a valid `clear=false`
measurement for each blocked placement; an invalid measurement aborts
immediately rather than being flattened into ordinary obstruction.
