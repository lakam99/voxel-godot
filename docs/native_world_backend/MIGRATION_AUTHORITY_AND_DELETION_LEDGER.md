# Native World Backend Authority And Deletion Ledger

This is the checked-in cutover ledger for N0–N9. A row may move to `deleted`
only after its named parity, integration, live, consumer-inventory, and
independent-review gates pass. A fallback is a temporary migration adapter, not
a permanent production mode.

Status vocabulary: `frozen`, `planned`, `shadow`, `cutover`, `deleted`,
`blocked`. Through the bounded N3 terrain/save milestone no production runtime
authority has advanced to `cutover` or `deleted`. The pure-core/plugin,
effective-terrain, and terrain-save-v2 slices are `shadow`: they are compiled,
tested, differentially checked, and adapter-smoked, but production generation,
terrain volume, save orchestration, publication, and collision remain with
their N0 owners.

## Mandatory receipt fields

Every stage receipt records:

- Git commit and clean/dirty state;
- complete first-party native source inventory and digest;
- dependency revision and cleanliness;
- compiler/linker/coverage-tool identity and effective flags;
- debug/release/coverage binary and installed-copy hashes;
- world identity, generator/recipe/schema versions, seed, coordinates, save
  identity, and runner arguments;
- queue limits, cancellation epoch, transaction/revision bindings, and artifact
  signatures relevant to the stage;
- functional result separately from cleanup result;
- authoritative zero-owned-process and zero-native-worker lifecycle proof;
- report/capture paths and a plain statement of what the evidence does and does
  not prove.

## Cutover review rule

Before each row changes to `cutover`, a development owner supplies the receipt
and an independent reviewer checks purity, parity oracle independence, coverage
denominator, source/binary identity, stale-result rejection, acknowledgement,
lifecycle, consumer inventory, and deletion patch. The cutover commit contains
the deletion or names the immediately following deletion commit; it may not
leave a hidden optional production fallback.
