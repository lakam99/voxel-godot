The claws pass only when every root is visually buried, the five lengths read as one biological hierarchy, the first half is gently directed rather than hooked, cross-sections are laterally compressed with dorsal/ventral asymmetry, the fan follows toe axes, and the ends look like worn keratin unguals instead of inserted spikes.
